# Marketing Knowledge Base (References)

This directory is the dedicated knowledge base for the `marketing` skill.

## How to Use
Drop any relevant files here. The marketing AI will read these files to understand your brand and product context before generating content.

## Recommended Files:
- `brand_guidelines.pdf`: Fonts, colors, tone of voice, and brand identity.
- `target_audience.md`: Buyer personas and demographic data.
- `competitor_analysis.csv`: Key competitors and their strategies.
- `product_features.md`: Detailed specs and unique selling propositions (USPs).
